Sheet 10Notes

Things brokers ask us.

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp. A person replies, usually within the hour.

N-01Is my data safe? Who owns it?

Your firm owns every lead, chat and number in Bayana. Agents see only their own leads and masked phone numbers, exports are owner-only and every export is logged. You can export everything to Excel whenever you want.

N-02My team isn’t very techy. Will they actually use it?

If they can use WhatsApp, they can use Bayana: chats, quick replies and templates work the way they expect. We set it up in your office, import your Excel sheets and train the team in one sitting, in Hindi or English.

N-03Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

We set up your firm’s official WhatsApp Business number with you, either a new number or your current business one, including Meta’s verification. Agents reply from Bayana, so buyers always see your firm’s name, not an agent’s personal number.

N-04Do you track my agents all day?

No. Bayana reads location only at the moment an agent taps check-in or check-out, to confirm they’re at the office or the property (within 200 m by default). There’s no live map and no route history.

N-05Which lead sources does it work with?

99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, Facebook and Instagram ads, your WhatsApp number, your website, walk-ins and referrals. Each lead shows where it came from, and you can see what each source really costs you per deal.

N-06What does it really cost?

The plan price plus 18% GST, which most brokerages can claim back. WhatsApp’s own message charges are passed on at Meta’s rate with no markup, usually ₹2,000–3,000 a month for a 15-person team. Masked calling is an optional add-on at cost.

N-07What happens in the founding pilot?

We’re onboarding the first 10 brokerages in Gurugram. You get 60 days free, setup and training in your office, and a direct line to the founders to shape what we build next. After that, the Team plan is ₹6,999 a month, locked for two years.

N-08Can I leave if it doesn’t work for us?

Yes. Plans are monthly with no contract. Export your leads, chats and notes to Excel and go.
